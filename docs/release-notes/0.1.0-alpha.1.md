# Zivlet 0.1.0-alpha.1

The first public alpha of Zivlet: a free, open-source file utility for macOS. Drag files in Finder, tap a key, and
drop them on a slice of a Liquid Glass wheel to convert, read or protect them.

> [!WARNING]
> **Alpha, and not notarized.** This build is signed ad-hoc, not with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS can't verify
> who made it. Every automated check passes, but hands-on testing by a person has not been done yet. Expect rough
> edges, and please [report what you find](https://github.com/Anmol-Sharma21/Zivlet/issues).

## Install

You need macOS 26 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.

1. Download `Zivlet-0.1.0-alpha.1.dmg`, open it, and drag **Zivlet** to **Applications**.
2. Open Zivlet. macOS says it can't verify the developer: click **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down to the message about Zivlet, and click **Open Anyway**.
   Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

You only do this once. The app lives in the menu bar.

## What you can do

- **Convert** images (JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, TIFF, AVIF, BMP and PDF output, with DNG and SVG as inputs), audio
  (MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, Ogg, Opus, AIFF, WMA) and video (MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI, WMV and animated GIF). Tap ⇧
  while dragging. Results are saved next to the originals, and you can cancel or undo.
- **Copy Text** from photos, screenshots, scans and PDFs, in English and Hindi, using on-device text recognition.
- **Read QR** codes and barcodes in images and PDFs.
- **Lock** a PDF with AES-256, with a suggested strong password and printing, copying and editing permissions.
  **Unlock** it or **change** its password.
- **Actions** on the ⌃ wheel: AirDrop, Copy to, Move to, Copy Path and Open in Terminal.
- **Drop Box**: a shelf at the notch for files you're gathering, with bulk share, AirDrop, copy, move and trash.
- **Progress and results at the notch**, with Undo, Show in Finder, Open and Copy. On a Mac without a notch, they
  appear at the top of the screen.

## Privacy

- Everything runs on your Mac. There is no account, no telemetry and no cloud service.
- This build makes no network requests at all. The updater stays off until signed releases exist, so get new
  versions from this Releases page.
- Zivlet only asks for access to the folders you choose. It never logs or stores passwords or the text it reads.

## Known limitations

- Not notarized (see above). A Developer ID build comes later.
- An SVG becomes a PDF page made from an image; vector PDFs from SVG are not available yet.
- GIFs made from long videos are very large; the result card says so when a clip is over a minute.
- Documents, spreadsheets, subtitles and archives can't be converted yet; they are on the roadmap.
