# Zivlet 0.1 (private alpha)

Draft. These notes are shown in the update window and on the release page. Edit them before the tag is pushed.

This is the first build of Zivlet for a small group of testers. Expect rough edges, and please tell us about them.

## What you can do

- Start dragging files in Finder, tap ⇧, ⌘ or ⌃, and drop them on a slice of the wheel to convert them. Results are
  saved next to the originals.
- Convert images (JPEG, PNG, HEIC, WebP, TIFF, AVIF, BMP, GIF and PDF, with SVG as an input), audio (MP3, M4A, WAV,
  FLAC, Ogg, Opus, AIFF) and video (MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI, GIF).
- Use the actions wheel to copy files somewhere, copy their paths, send them with AirDrop, move them, or open their
  folder in a terminal.
- Watch progress, cancel, undo a conversion or a move, and reveal results from the menu bar.
- Choose your keys, sounds, haptics and where results go when a folder is read-only, in Settings. Zivlet can start
  at login.

## Good to know

- Everything runs on your Mac: your files never leave it. There is no account and no telemetry; the only network
  request is the update check, which you can turn off in Settings › Updates.
- Zivlet needs macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon.
- The only permission Zivlet asks for is access to folders you choose.

## Known limitations

- Vector PDFs from SVG files are not available yet; an SVG becomes a PDF page made from an image.
- GIFs made from long videos are very large; the result card says so when a clip is over a minute.
